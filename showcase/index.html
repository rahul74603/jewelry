    FAQs

    Price kaise banta hai?

    Gold rate (per gram) × weight + making charges + GST. Rate roz badalta hai, isliye aaj ka rate calculator me daal kar estimate nikalo.

    22K aur 18K me kya farak hai?

    22K me zyada gold hota hai (916 purity), 18K thoda halka aur zyada mazboot hota hai — daily wear aur stone wale designs me zyada use hota hai.

    Old gold exchange hota hai?

    Shop se confirm karna baaki hai. Aam taur par purane gold ka weight aur purity check karke us din ke rate par exchange hota hai.

    Order kaise karein?
