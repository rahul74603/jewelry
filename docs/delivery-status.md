**Scope choices:** Basic/default = one device. The named Standard example starts at two, Premium at five; the provisioned license stores its actual limit. No fake seven-day lease: the live pilot is online-only. The demo is intentionally not a licensed business database. The live workspace does not cache financial records in browser storage. Changing a shop name or a rate affects future documents, not historical snapshots.

**Verification:** `npm test` covers precision, mixed metals, tax dates, payments, discounts, stock, reversals, expiry and token/device/tenant policies. Browser tests exercise mobile layout/navigation, sample reset, settings, draft → final → reversal, stock adjustments, search and customer creation. The separate emulator job exercises authenticated callables with two real emulator identities, direct rules denials, foreign tokens, single-use proofs, concurrent activation, revocation after challenge issuance, transfer, renewal, idempotent postings and rollback. See the actual Actions checks for remote results; adding tests is not a production certification.
